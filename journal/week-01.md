# Week 01 Engineering Journal

## What I worked on

This week I learned the basic AI landscape and practiced distinguishing AI, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, and AI agents.

I also learned how an LLM generates responses and why AI outputs need to be verified.

## What I practiced

- Distinguished AI from traditional automation
- Compared AI assistants with search and authoritative references
- Performed an AI comparison using the same question
- Practiced checking AI claims against independent sources
- Classified examples as prediction, classification, or generation
- Created a personal AI verification protocol

## What I learned

One important lesson was that an AI answer can sound confident and still need verification. I should not treat agreement between two AI assistants as proof that a claim is correct.

## What I found difficult

Understanding the differences between LLMs, RAG systems, tool-using assistants, and AI agents was initially confusing.

## What I will do differently next time

I will check important claims against reliable sources and record how I verified them instead of relying only on the AI response.

## Reflection

Week 1 helped me understand that using AI is not only about getting an answer. I also need to inspect, verify, document, and reflect on the result.
