# AI Comparison - Week 01

## Question Used

> What is the boiling point of pure water at standard atmospheric pressure, and what happens to the boiling point when atmospheric pressure decreases?

## AI Assistants Compared

| Assistant | Main answer | Agreement / Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Gemini | Pure water boils at about 100°C at 1 atm. Lower atmospheric pressure lowers the boiling point. | Agreed with Claude on the main claim. |
| Claude | Pure water boils at about 100°C at 1 atm. Lower pressure lowers the boiling point. It also explained vapor pressure and gave altitude examples. | Agreed with Gemini on the main claim, with additional explanation. |

## Reference Check

I compared both AI answers with the NIST reference used in Q4.

Reference:
https://webbook.nist.gov/cgi/cbook.cgi?ID=C7732185&Mask=224

The main claim from both assistants agreed with the reference.

## What I Learned

The two AI assistants gave similar answers, but agreement between AI systems is not enough to prove that a claim is correct. I should compare important claims with an independent reliable source.
